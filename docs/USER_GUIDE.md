# IdeaBloom user guide

_Your ideas, in full bloom._ IdeaBloom helps you catch ideas, grow the good ones into projects, plan them with
tasks and milestones, and keep notes and useful links next to them.

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** (name, email, a password of at least 12 characters).
2. You land on the **Dashboard**. Type an idea in **Capture an idea…** and press Enter. That's it: details can wait.
3. Use the menu on the left to move between sections. On a phone, open it with the ☰ button.

**Tip:** press **Ctrl + K** (Cmd + K on a Mac) anywhere to search everything.

## Dashboard

Your day at a glance: active ideas, active projects, pending and **overdue** tasks (red), deadlines for the next
14 days, projects in progress with their progress bars, ideas by status, tasks finished per week, recent activity
and your newest ideas. You can capture an idea or add a task right from here.

## Ideas

- **Capture** quickly, then open an idea to add a description, category, tags, priority and links.
- **Status**: Inbox → Exploring → Planned. **Favourite** (⭐) the ones you care about.
- **Archive** ideas you're not working on (you can restore them); **delete** removes one for good.
- **Convert to project** when an idea is ready: the project starts with the idea's details.
- **Organise** (Ideas → Organise): manage categories (with colours) and tags.

## Projects and milestones

- A project has goals, dates (start, target), a status and a priority.
- **Progress** comes from finished tasks (or, without tasks, from finished milestones). A completed project is 100%.
- **Milestones** are checkpoints: tick them off, reorder them with the arrows.
- Link related ideas; see the project's history under **Activity**.

## Tasks

- **Pending / Overdue / Completed / All** at the top of the Tasks page; filter by project or priority, search, sort.
- Tick the box to complete a task. Completing a task also completes its open **subtasks**.
- Click a task's title to edit it in the side panel (dates, priority, notes, project).
- "Overdue" uses your time zone (Settings → Profile).

## Notes

- **New note** opens the editor: headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code, links.
- Notes **save automatically** about a second after you stop typing ("Saving… → Saved").
- Pin important notes, add tags and a category, or attach a note to an idea or project.

## Resources

Saved links (articles, videos, documents…) with your own notes. Paste a link and IdeaBloom suggests the type
(YouTube → video, PDF → document). Links open in a new tab.

## Calendar

Every date in one month view: task due dates (including subtasks), milestones, and project start and target
dates. Overdue items are red, finished ones are faded with a tick. Click a task to edit it; click anything else to
open its project. On a phone, the calendar becomes a day-by-day list.

## Search

**Ctrl + K** or the search box in the top bar: results appear as you type, grouped by type; use the arrow keys and
Enter. **See all results** opens the search page, where you can show one type at a time or include archived ideas.
Partial words work: "gard" finds "garden".

## Settings

- **Profile**: name, bio, time zone.
- **Password**: changing it signs out your other devices.
- **Appearance & preferences**: Light, Dark or System theme (follows your device), and the page that opens after
  you log in.
- **Signed-in devices**: see where you're logged in and sign any device out (or all of them).
- **Your data**: download everything you've created as one file.
- **Delete account**: permanently removes your account and everything in it (asks for your password).

## Keyboard

| Keys                        | Does                                    |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Ctrl + K                    | Open search                             |
| Ctrl + B                    | Show/hide the side menu                 |
| Tab, at the top of any page | "Skip to content"                       |
| Enter                       | Save a quick capture, a task, a subtask |
